### 等价的条件
定义$$l(a) = \begin{cases} 0, & a \text{ 可逆}, \\ s, & a = p_1 p_2 \cdots p_s, \; p_i \text{ 为素元数}. \end{cases}$$
![Pasted image 20260226153841](Pasted%20image%2020260226153841.png)

这个定义说明la的元素组成个数
由此产生的三个性质




>[!property of l(a)] 
>
>    If $a$ and $b$ are associates, then $l(a) = l(b)$.
 >   If $a|b$, but $b \nmid a$, then $l(a) &lt; l(b)$.>   
 >   If $a|b$, then $l(a) = l(b)$ if and only if $a$ and $b$ are associates.
